Report definitions and data coverage

Retention: distinct members with a non-cancelled membership covering the period end divided by distinct members with a membership covering the period start. It is not a renewal prediction.

Attendance percentage: average daily attendance visits divided by the current active-member count. Member schedules and expected visit frequency are not stored.

Financials: collected revenue counts only Success transactions. Net collection subtracts full refunds recorded within the selected period. GST totals use stored tax-invoice snapshots only and are not official GST returns.

Trainer/staff attendance is unavailable because the system stores member attendance only.

Salary paid/pending reports are unavailable because salary transactions are not recorded.

Membership cancellation reasons and cancellation percentages are unavailable because cancellation history is not stored.

Biometric attendance is only reportable if attendance records already contain that source; no device integration is performed here.
